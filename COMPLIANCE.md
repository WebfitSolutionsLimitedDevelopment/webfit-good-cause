# Good Cause compliance architecture

## Before campaign activation

- campaign owner identity and contact details
- beneficiary identity or organisation details
- beneficiary consent where fundraising for someone else
- intended use of funds
- supporting evidence proportionate to risk
- bank or payout destination verification
- moderation decision and audit trail

## Higher-risk review

Enhanced review should be considered for international beneficiaries, disaster zones, unusual payment patterns, high-value campaigns, complex organisations, sanctions exposure, PEP indicators, unclear beneficiary relationships and urgent payout requests.

## Payout controls

No payout to an unverified payee. Maintain a reconciliation trail from gross donations to payment-provider costs, Good Cause fees, refunds, chargebacks, reserves and net campaign proceeds.

## Public claims

Do not publish claims of Stripe approval, charity registration, donee status, tax-credit eligibility, AML/CFT reporting-entity status or regulatory endorsement unless verified and current.
