# Good Cause payment and payout model

All supporter card payments are processed through the Webfit Solutions Limited Stripe account.

Fundraisers and beneficiaries do not connect payment processor accounts. All card processing remains under Webfit Solutions Limited.

After a successful contribution, Good Cause records the gross contribution, actual Stripe processing fee, 2.5% Good Cause platform fee and net amount payable to the campaign. The net amount becomes a pending payout liability.

Authorised Good Cause finance/admin staff verify the beneficiary bank destination, make the beneficiary bank transfer, record the bank transfer reference in the admin payout ledger and mark the payout as paid.
